Finance · Quant

Multi-sector equity crash study

Automated financial pipeline in R using tidyquant to calculate max drawdown, crash beta, and recovery time for 47 equities across 6 market sectors. Compared sector behavior across two distinct 2019–2023 crash regimes: the COVID-19 demand shock and the 2022 inflation-driven bear market.

Key finding Healthcare was the most consistent protector across both downturns, while Energy and Tech completely reversed their performance rankings between the demand-driven and inflation-driven crashes — proof that "defensive" sector labels don't hold up across every type of crash.

ML · Team project

NBA player performance — height & 3-point shooting across eras

Led a team of 4 to ingest and clean 6,000 player-seasons spanning three historical eras (1996–2024) using the NBA API, building reproducible Python pipelines that cut manual data-cleaning time by 10+ hours. Modeled the shifting relationship between physical traits and 3-point efficiency.

Key finding The "stretch big" archetype is real: three-point attempts among centers/power forwards surged 1,906% from 1996–2024 — outpacing even Guards and Wings — while height remained a statistically significant negative predictor of 3-point efficiency across all three eras.
